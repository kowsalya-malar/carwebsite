McLaren is a British automotive company renowned for producing high-performance supercars and hypercars that combine Formula 1 technology with luxury design. Founded in 1963 by racer Bruce McLaren, the brand first gained fame in Formula 1 before entering the road car market with the legendary McLaren F1 in 1992.
